# StorySaver — Privacy Policy

Public privacy policy for the Android app **StorySaver** (`com.sriram.storyproject`).

Live page:

https://sriramnatrajan.github.io/storysaver-privacy/

Use that URL in Google Play Console → App content → Privacy policy, and as the privacy policy URL in the AdMob app settings.

The page covers the on-device Instagram session, saved files, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, and Google AdMob ads. Analytics does not include Instagram usernames. The app is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
